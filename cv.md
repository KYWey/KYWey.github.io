<section class="cv-card">
  <h2>Research Summary</h2>
  <div class="cv-summary">
    <h3>Visualizing electric-field-induced deformation and depinning of a charge density wave at the atomic scale</h3>
    <p>My main thesis project uses current-tuned STM — a scanning tunneling microscope with electrical
    contacts on the sample — to image the charge density wave in the blue bronze
    K<sub>0.3</sub>MoO<sub>3</sub> while an in-plane electric field is applied across it. The
    manuscript is in preparation; the main findings are:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>We establish a new scanned-probe platform for studying correlated quantum matter under a
      drive current.</li>
      <li>We present a recipe for high-resolution imaging of blue bronze materials, which have
      complicated molecular structures.</li>
      <li>Geometric phase analysis of the CDW shows how its dislocations evolve at different in-plane
      electric fields.</li>
      <li>The surface CDW is much more strongly pinned than the CDW in the bulk — direct microscopic
      confirmation of the defect-mediated depinning picture introduced by Lee and Rice decades ago.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>
