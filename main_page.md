## What I do

I work at the [Quantum Matter Design Studio (QMDS)](https://sites.google.com/g.ucla.edu/quantum/)
at UCLA. My research focuses on **low-dimensional materials** — crystals so thin or so
"one-directional" that quantum mechanics takes over and produces surprising electronic behavior.

To study them, I use two powerful microscopes-of-a-kind:

<ul class="chips">
  <li class="chip">🔬 Scanning Tunneling Microscopy (STM)</li>
  <li class="chip">📈 Angle-Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy (ARPES)</li>
</ul>

STM lets me image individual atoms on a surface, while ARPES maps out how electrons are allowed
to move through a material. My current project probes the **atomic-scale dynamics of a quasi-1D
charge density wave** by running an electrical current through the sample and watching, atom by atom,
how the electronic order responds.

Curious what any of that means? I wrote a friendly, jargon-light explainer on the
[Research page →]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})
