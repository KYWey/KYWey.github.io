# Pet Life: Meet Cooby 🐶

<p class="lead">I used to call myself a 50/50 dog-and-cat person. Then, on <strong>2024/10/17</strong>,
<strong>Cooby</strong> — literally "Cool Baby" — joined our family, and now I'm 100% a dog person.</p>

After my sister and I both left home for our PhDs, we figured our parents could use some company —
and our mom had always wanted a dog. Even our dad, who was a little afraid of dogs growing up, fell
for Cooby the moment he met him. 💙 I was lucky enough to visit home and spend time with Cooby when
he was about two months and again around eight months old.
