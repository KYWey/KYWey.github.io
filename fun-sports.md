# Sport Life 🏓🧗🥾

<p class="lead">Staying active keeps me sane through the PhD. Here are the three sports that fill
my evenings and weekends.</p>

<div class="feature">
  <h3>🏓 Table Tennis</h3>
  <p>I picked up table tennis in high school and got hooked in undergrad, where I had the honor of
  serving as the Physics Department team captain. Since joining UCLA in 2021 I've kept playing with
  the club and its junior varsity team, competing (and scrimmaging) against other universities.
  Not a pro — but definitely not a rookie.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
  <h3>🧗 Climbing &amp; Bouldering</h3>
  <p>Mostly indoor bouldering — climbing short, rope-free routes that are equal parts strength,
  technique, and puzzle-solving. I climb around <strong>V6–V8</strong> at SenderOne Westwood. I'm a
  little scared of heights, so you won't catch me on the tall roped routes; bouldering is the sweet
  spot, and it's a fantastic way to make friends. I share clips of my climbs on
  <a href="https://www.xiaohongshu.com/user/profile/641589970000000011021c49?xsec_token=YB4Rr4V3UcosH2iCwq3lZmizs3eJI2Bg4htBaJfUpVwg4=&xsec_source=app_share&xhsshare=CopyLink&appuid=641589970000000011021c49&apptime=1755455774&share_id=eb1584bcfa9e4770ba0e89f276a8a8d5" target="_blank" rel="noopener">XiaoHongShu</a>
  (in Mandarin, but the climbing speaks for itself — feel free to stop by!).</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
  <h3>🥾 Outdoor Hiking</h3>
  <p>Southern California is a hiker's playground, and nothing resets my brain after a long lab week
  like a trail and a big view. I'm always collecting new routes around LA and beyond — trip reports
  and photos coming soon.</p>
</div>
